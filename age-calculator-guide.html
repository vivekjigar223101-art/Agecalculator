What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates the difference between a person's date of birth and another selected date.

Instead of manually counting years, months and days, you can enter the dates into an age calculator and get the result quickly.

Age calculators are useful for checking your current age, calculating age on a particular date and understanding the exact difference between two dates.

How to Calculate Age?

Your age is generally calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date.

Simple Example

Suppose a person was born on 10 January 2000. To calculate the person's age, compare the date of birth with the date on which the calculation is being made.

The difference can then be expressed in years, months and days.

When calculating manually, you also need to consider whether the birthday has already occurred in the current year.
