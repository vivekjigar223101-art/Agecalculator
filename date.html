Date Calculator – Calculate Date Difference Easily

Our free Date Calculator helps you calculate the difference between two dates and find the total number of days between them. You can also add or subtract a specific number of days from a date.

How to Use the Date Calculator?

  1. Select the first date.
  2. Select the second date.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. Your date difference and total days will be displayed.

What Can You Calculate?

  • Difference between two dates
  • Total number of days between dates
  • Add days to a date
  • Subtract days from a date
  • Calculate dates in years, months and days

Why Use an Online Date Calculator?

Calculating the exact number of days between two dates manually can be difficult, especially when different months have different numbers of days. An online Date Calculator makes the calculation quick and convenient.
