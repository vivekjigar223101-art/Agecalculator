What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol used for percentage is %.

For example, 25% means 25 out of 100, or 25/100.

How to Calculate Percentage?

To find what percentage one number is of another, use the following basic formula:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example

Suppose you scored 45 marks out of 60.

Percentage = (45 ÷ 60) × 100

Percentage = 75%

Therefore, 45 out of 60 is 75%.

How to Calculate Percentage Increase?

Percentage increase tells you how much a value has increased compared with its original value.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example

Suppose a price increases from ₹100 to ₹120.

Increase = ₹120 − ₹100 = ₹20

Percentage Increase = (20 ÷ 100) × 100

Percentage Increase = 20%

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease?

Percentage decrease shows how much a value has decreased compared with its original value.

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example

Suppose a price decreases from ₹500 to ₹400.

Decrease = ₹500 − ₹400 = ₹100

Percentage Decrease = (100 ÷ 500) × 100

Percentage Decrease = 20%
