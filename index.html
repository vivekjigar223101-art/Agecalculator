Age Calculator – Calculate Your Exact Age

Our free Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age in years, months and days. Simply enter your date of birth and calculate your age instantly.

How to Use the Age Calculator?

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. Your exact age will be displayed instantly.

What Can You Calculate?

  • Exact age in years, months and days
  • Total number of days
  • Day of birth
  • Next birthday information

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this Age Calculator?

The calculator uses your entered dates to calculate the difference between the dates and provides the result automatically.

Can I calculate my age for a future date?

Yes. You can select a future date to see how old you will be on that date.

Is this Age Calculator free?
