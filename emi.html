How is EMI Calculated?

EMI is generally calculated using the loan amount, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly installments.

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + R)ⁿ − 1)

Here, P represents the principal loan amount, R represents the monthly interest rate and n represents the total number of monthly installments.
