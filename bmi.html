How to Use the BMI Calculator?

  1. Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
  3. Click the Calculate BMI button.
  4. Your BMI value and category will appear instantly.

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 – Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 – Normal Weight
  • 25 to 29.9 – Overweight
  • 30 or above – Obesity

How is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing a person's weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this BMI Calculator free?

Yes, this online BMI Calculator is free to use.

What information do I need?

You only need your height in centimeters and weight in kilograms to calculate BMI.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measure and does not by itself diagnose a person's health condition. For personal health decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
