What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a value calculated using a person's weight and height.

BMI can provide a general indication of body size relative to height, but it does not directly measure body fat and does not account for every aspect of individual health.

How to Calculate BMI?

BMI is calculated using weight in kilograms and height in meters.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Example

Suppose a person weighs 70 kg and has a height of 1.75 m.

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75)

BMI ≈ 22.86

The calculated BMI is approximately 22.86.

BMI Categories for Adults

For adults, BMI values are commonly grouped into categories used for screening purposes.

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30.0 or higher

These categories are general screening ranges and should not be treated as a complete assessment of an individual's health.

Important Note About BMI

BMI is a screening measurement rather than a diagnosis. It does not distinguish between muscle mass, fat mass, bone density and other factors.

Children, teenagers, pregnant people and some athletes may require different approaches to interpreting body-size measurements.
