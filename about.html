About Us

Welcome to Age Calculator, a simple and free online tool designed to help users calculate their exact age quickly and easily.

Our Age Calculator can calculate your age in years, months and days. It can also provide information such as total days lived, day of birth and your next birthday.

Our goal is to provide a simple, useful and easy-to-use calculator that works smoothly on mobile phones, tablets and computers.

We are continuously working to improve this website and may add more useful calculators and tools in the future.
